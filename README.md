# SAAFI Boutique Hotel

Bespoke luxury boutique hotel showcase and direct concierge booking platform for SAAFI Boutique Hotel, No. 6 Madiana Close, Off Dar es Salaam Crescent, Wuse 2, Abuja.

## Architecture
- `index.html`: Flagship landing showcase and integrated availability bar
- `rooms.html`: Accommodation catalog of all 22 keys across 4 suites
- `rooms/signature-room.html`: Signature Room suite experience and direct booking
- `rooms/deluxe-plus.html`: Deluxe Plus suite experience and direct booking
- `rooms/deluxe-royal.html`: Deluxe Royal suite experience and direct booking
- `rooms/executive-suite.html`: Executive Suite experience and direct booking
- `dining.html`: Restaurant, private dining, breakfast, and room service menu
- `meetings.html`: Executive Boardroom and conference packages (₦250k/day)
- `contact.html`: Concierge, arrival directions, and airport chauffeur transfers
- `styles.css`: Warm stone and noir luxury design system
- `app.js`: Interactive booking calculations, spring animations, and mobile drawer

African & Continental Dining

From our complimentary morning breakfast table to chef-crafted evening dinners, indulge in authentic Nigerian dishes and continental favorites.

COMPLIMENTARY DAILY

The Morning Breakfast Table

Every room reservation at SAAFI Boutique Hotel includes our freshly prepared daily breakfast, served from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Guests can choose from full English, continental, American, and Halal options.

Full English / Continental: Eggs made to order, baked beans, sausages, toasted bread, butter, jam, and fresh fruits.
Nigerian Morning Staples: Yam and eggs, plantain frittata, tea, and brewed rich coffee.
Beverage Bar: Fresh juice, tea selection, and rich brewed coffee.
